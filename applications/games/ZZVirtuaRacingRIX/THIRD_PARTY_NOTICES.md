- `cpu/drc/emit_arm.c`, `emith_xbranch()`: a far-call sequence under

## VirtuaRacingDeluxeEnhanced (`Core1/vrde/`, hooks inside `third_party/picodrive/`)

Copyright (c) 2026 PokaMilkTea, revision `9f059e10e4c8bff928a581777f19e80c3d117b92`
(1.2.0). Licence: the same terms as PicoDrive (`Core1/vrde/LICENSE_VRDE.txt`,
`third_party/LICENSE_VRDE.txt`).

- `hle.c/h`, `sh2raster.c/h`, `hlecheck.c/h`: unmodified, except the two
  `fprintf(stderr)` of `hle.c`, which became counters (`zzvr_hle_note`,
  `Core1/vrde/zzvr_hle_diag.h`).
  ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A7b adds to `hle.c/h` `hle_set_light_lists()` (a scene
  from the 68000 then keeps only its 3D lines, no polygon list) and
  `hle_interpolated_builds()` (a counter of the lists `hle_interpolated()`
  built), marked "ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A7b".
  ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A8b: the depth buckets' counts cleared instead of the whole
  800 KB structure, the geometry state without its vertex array (both written
  before they are read: identical lists), and the wheels' strobe in
  `interpolate()` (`hle_wheel_strobe`), marked "ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A8b"; A8c:
  the wheels' speed-driven turn and blurred copy (`wheel_blur`,
  `draw_records()`, `emit()`), marked "ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A8c"; A8g: `divide()`
  without the 64-bit library division, `sh2_word()` inlined, `expand.c`'s
  neighbour indices without a modulo, `sky.c` `sky_line_rows()`, marked
  "ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A8g".
- `sky.c/h` (A8b): plane B drawn from the VDP's memory (`draw_plane_b_wide()`
  of VRDE's `layers.c`), its scroll following the interpolated camera
  (`note_sky_scroll()` / `sky_offset_for()` of `vrde.c`) and its dither blended
  (`skyblend_shader` of `vrde.c`, in integer arithmetic on the CPU).
- `hud.c/h`: `hud_note_sh2_access()` (fed by the SH-2 interpreter's access hook)
  replaced by `hud_note_dram_write()`, fed by a frame buffer write hook that
  the recompiler's writes go through too (`zzvr_dram_write_hook`, added to
  `pico/pico.h` and to the three `sh2_write*_dram` handlers of
  `pico/32x/memory.c`, marked `ZZVirtuaRacingRIX`). A3a adds `hud_keep_row()`
  (`hud_keep_framebuffer_pixel()` for a whole line).
- `expand.c/h` (A3a): `expand_polygon()` of VRDE's `vrde.c`, unchanged but for
  the vertex type (x, y and, from A8a, the alpha that carries the haze).

## librixgpu (`Core1/librixgpu/`)

Copyright (c) 2026 Xanxi, 0BSD (`Core1/librixgpu/LICENSE_0BSD.txt`), version
1.5 as in ZZOpenLaraRIX, plus one addition for ZZVirtuaRacingRIX A7a (a PL330
