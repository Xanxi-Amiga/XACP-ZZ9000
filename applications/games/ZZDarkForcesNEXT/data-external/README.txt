External TFE data files
=======================

Copy these files into the Amiga Data drawer alongside the original Dark Forces data:

    weapons.json
    projectiles.json
    effects.json
    pickups.json
    TfeMessages.txt

They come from the pinned The Force Engine source tree and are required by the engine.
They are not original commercial Dark Forces game data.

The original GOB/LFD files are not included and must come from a legally owned copy of the game.
