NASA imagery used by ZZScreensaverE
=====================================

ZZScreensaverE embeds a 1024x512 RGB565 derivative of the NASA Visible Earth
image:

"The Blue Marble: Land Surface, Ocean Color, Sea Ice and Clouds"

Original source image used for the texture lineage:
land_ocean_ice_cloud_2048.jpg
2048x1024 pixels
NASA Visible Earth record 57735
Published 8 February 2002

Source page:
https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57735/the-blue-marble-land-surface-ocean-color-sea-ice-and-clouds

Direct source image:
https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57735/land_ocean_ice_cloud_2048.jpg

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center

Enhancements by Robert Simmon (ocean color, compositing, 3D globes,
animation)

Team, MODIS Atmosphere Group, MODIS Ocean Group

Flagstaff Field Center
